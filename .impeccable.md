## Design Context

### Users
DJs, artists, event organizers, venue owners, brand managers in the underground/electronic music scene. Primary audience is artists aged 18–35 wanting professional management and visibility. Secondary audience is bookers, venues, and brands seeking authentic partnerships.

### Brand Personality
Electric · Precise · Underground. Witty, sharp, modern tone. Confident and energetic messaging. Trend-aware, culturally attuned, never corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction (rebuild — single-purple editorial)
Dark — this product is consumed in the world of electronic music: clubs, studios, late nights. The homepage rebuild commits to ONE accent: electric purple `#9D14FF` on near-black `#120917` (purple-tinted) with white ink. No cyan, no lime, no red — a single disciplined accent. Photo treatment is hybrid: artist portraits sit dark/desaturated at rest and warm to full color on interaction; atmosphere/show imagery uses a purple duotone.

Anti-references: anything that looks like a generic SaaS, "tech startup", or template landing page.

> Legacy note: earlier guidelines mandated a purple+cyan pair and four per-style palettes. The rebuild supersedes that with the single-purple system above. The legacy color tokens remain defined in `globals.css`/`tailwind.config.js` only so the (untouched) bookings page keeps rendering; the homepage uses only the new tokens.

### Design Principles
1. **One accent, held with discipline** — electric purple only; everything else is near-black + white
2. **Typography pairing**: Bebas Neue (display, all-caps headers) + Space Grotesk (body/UI)
3. **No AI tells** — no border-left accent stripes, no gradient text, no same-padding-everywhere layouts, no SaaS metric bars, no 3-equal-card rows; prefer hairline dividers, asymmetry, negative space, framed monochrome logo tiles
4. **Dark by design** — not dark as a trend, dark because the product lives in clubs and studios
5. **Motion on intent** — auto-cycling/expanding panels (Roster, What We Do), hover/scroll reveals, glow via inner edge-refraction not neon; all gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`
