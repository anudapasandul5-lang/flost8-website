## Design Context

### Users
DJs, artists, event organizers, venue owners, brand managers in the underground/electronic music scene. Primary audience is artists aged 18–35 wanting professional management and visibility. Secondary audience is bookers, venues, and brands seeking authentic partnerships.

### Brand Personality
Electric · Precise · Underground. Witty, sharp, modern tone. Confident and energetic messaging. Trend-aware, culturally attuned, never corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark — this product is consumed in the world of electronic music: clubs, studios, late nights. The brand mandates four distinct visual styles (Performance/Live Sets, Artist Identity, Content/Media Engine, Cultural Collaborations), each with its own color palette. The dominant visual language uses the Performance palette: deep near-black (#090808), electric purple (#B343F8), and cyan (#77F4F4).

Anti-references: anything that looks like a generic SaaS, "tech startup", or template landing page.

### Design Principles
1. **Brand-mandated colors are non-negotiable** — the four palette systems come directly from the brand guidelines PDF
2. **Typography pairing**: Bebas Neue (display, all-caps headers) + Chakra Petch (body — tech/electronic edge)
3. **No AI tells** — avoid border-left accent stripes, gradient text, same-padding-everywhere layouts, hero metric bars
4. **Dark by design** — not dark as a trend, dark because the product lives in clubs and studios
5. **Glitch sparingly** — the glitch animation on the hero title fires infrequently, which is what makes it feel real rather than noisy
