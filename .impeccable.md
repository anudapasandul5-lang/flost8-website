## Design Context

### Users
DJs, artists, event organizers, venue owners, brand managers in the underground/electronic music scene. Primary audience is artists aged 18–35 wanting professional management and visibility. Secondary audience is bookers, venues, and brands seeking authentic partnerships.

### Brand Personality
Electric · Precise · Underground. Witty, sharp, modern tone. Confident and energetic messaging. Trend-aware, culturally attuned, never corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retro-editorial poster — a navy (#302A66) base with cream (#F5F0E8) sections, not a dark near-black club palette. Purple (#AE43F7), cyan (#70F0F2), red (#F20D0D), and lime (#CCFF00) appear as accents layered over the navy/cream foundation. The look reads like a printed music-zine or gig poster: bold cropped type, photographic textures, and confident color blocking — energetic without leaning on dark/neon club tropes.

Typography is a four-way pairing:
- **Bebas Neue** — display headings, condensed and punchy
- **League Gothic** — oversized editorial type, ghost outline words (text-outline-cream / text-outline-navy)
- **Hyperwave** — script accents for brand-voice words and signature moments
- **Circe** (incl. italic) — body copy, often set in italic for emphasis

Color ratio target: 60% navy + cream as the structural base, 30% purple + cyan as supporting accents, 10% red + lime reserved for rare, high-impact punches (CTAs, highlights).

Anti-references: anything that looks like a generic SaaS, "tech startup", or template landing page.

### Design Principles
1. **Brand-mandated colors are non-negotiable** — the four palette systems come directly from the brand guidelines PDF
2. **Editorial-bold** — broken/asymmetric grids, oversized type bleeding off-screen, varied section rhythm (alternate tight and generous spacing rather than uniform padding), and a left-aligned bias over centered compositions
3. **No AI tells** — no border-left/right accent stripes over 1px, no gradient text, no glassmorphism-everywhere, no identical icon-card grids, no bounce/elastic easing, animate only transform/opacity/clip-path (never layout properties)
4. **60-30-10 color discipline** — navy/cream carries the page, purple/cyan support, red/lime punctuate sparingly
5. **Glitch sparingly** — the glitch animation on the hero title fires infrequently, which is what makes it feel real rather than noisy
